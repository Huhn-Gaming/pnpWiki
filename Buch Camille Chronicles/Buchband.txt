Eternal light and shadow reign over the world of Dilaril since the Ceising, when Gods had vanished and had left behind a world of turmoil and pain.
But as always, the people had survived, forging on were the burn of the sun was a test of endurance to barely enough to survive.
Here, Harmonists like Camill of the Valleys use their powers of musical Melodies to shape the powers that had once killed another soul. 
She herself gets dragged into schemes and plots, seemingly foreseen and acted on by fate which drive her deeper into poverty. And the Chasm.
Now she has to dig herself from its fangs and rise again.
Her pride demands it.
